The Arbitrage Strategy — Buying Below Market Comp Prices

The core idea: buy a unit where the asking price is below what identical units in that building have recently sold for. The gap is immediate paper equity — you are acquiring at a discount to the market's own revealed price.

  • Entry signal: Asking PSF < median sale PSF from Jan 2025+, same building + beds (sqft-matched where possible)
  • Hold & earn: Rent the unit at 4–7% gross yield (typical for JVC / DSC / Dubai South), collect income while the price gap closes
  • Exit options: Resell at market once the gap narrows, or hold as a long-term income asset
  • Risk — off-plan: Pre-completion listings lock capital until handover. Verify the developer's track record and completion date
  • Risk — comp quality: Focus on green-dot buildings (20+ comps) for the most reliable market benchmarks
  • 🔥 Motivated seller flag: 53 of the 600 listings contain language like “distress sale”, “urgent”, “must sell”, or “relocating” in the listing text. These sellers may accept offers below asking, amplifying the discount further.
